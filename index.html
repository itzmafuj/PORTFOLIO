About Me

I'm Mafuj Hossain, a developer and the founder of Mfzx.Build.

I live in Gouria village, Malda district. I keep my roots close and my eyes on tech, and I believe in hard work, curiosity and building good connections.

    I started Mfzx.Build to make web and app products for a Gen Z audience that lives on its phone. I write the code and make the product calls.

    React on the front, Node.js, Express, Socket.io and MongoDB behind it. Smaller tools run on Firebase or Flask.
